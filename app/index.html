    Roadmap to platinum

    One playthrough, about 200 hours, difficulty 4/10. No online trophies, no mission replay, and cheats disable trophies and saving — so play it clean.

    1. Stage 1 — Play the story 60–80 hours

      Finish all 5 chapters and the final mission. That alone pops 12 story trophies plus both golds' groundwork. Two things matter while you play: meditate with Lucia on the Chapter 1 balcony (see Missables — it can be lost), and scan vehicles and study animals whenever you pass them, because the 100% checklist counts them later.

      Keep a manual save at the start of every chapter. The game gives you 15 manual slots and 1 autosave, and there is no mission replay.

    2. Stage 2 — The 100% checklist ~100 hours

      After the story, open Progress > Stats > 100% Completion. It is forgiving — closer to 60% true completion. You need any 100 optional missions, 200 of 277 vehicles scanned, 100 of 255 animals studied, 70 of 80 challenges, all 8 large bank jobs, all 39 gang operations, all 15 target decks, 300 stunts, every drone race and every shooting-range gold. Everything on it stays available in free-roam.

    3. Stage 3 — Cleanup ~10 hours

      Finish the 8 Strangers & Freaks strands, sweep up leftover collectibles and activities, then check the five unsolved trophies. Five trophies had no confirmed unlock in any published guide when this guide was written — they are flagged UNSOLVED in the list rather than guessed at.

    Sources: PowerPyx trophy guide & roadmap, PlanetGTA6 trophy list. Difficulty and time estimates are PowerPyx's.

    Missables

    Only 1 missable trophy

    Positive Mental Attitude

    Meditate at every spot across Leonida.

    There are 18 meditation spots across 10 locations, and each counts twice if both Jason and Lucia meditate there. The catch is the very first one: during Chapter 1, after the mission Down to Earth, talk to Lucia in the kitchen in the morning. She walks out to the balcony to meditate. Follow her and choose Accept. That meditation is gone for good after Chapter 1, and it is still unconfirmed whether the trophy needs it — so do it while you can.

    Everything else is safe: every other trophy is confirmed obtainable after the story in free-roam, and the five unsolved trophies have no confirmed missable status. The real safety net is manual saves — 15 slots, one at the start of each chapter.
