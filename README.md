# Leonida Legend — GTA VI Trophy Guide & Tracker

An offline, open-source guide and tracker for all **55 Grand Theft Auto VI
trophies** on PlayStation 5: 1 platinum, 2 gold, 6 silver, 46 bronze, 28 of
them hidden until earned.

## What it does

- **Checklist** — every trophy with its official name, description and grade,
  saved in the browser (localStorage), with export to JSON and import back.
- **Filters** — by kind (story, activities, collectibles, robberies, money,
  world, misc), grade, earned/remaining, free-text search, and missable-only.
- **Spoiler-safe** — story trophies stay hidden (name, description and guide)
  in the list, in the "Next up" suggestion and in the detail pane until you
  earn them or turn spoilers off.
- **Roadmap to platinum** — three stages, about 200 hours, difficulty 4/10.
- **Missables** — exactly one: *Positive Mental Attitude* (the Chapter 1
  balcony meditation). Everything else is obtainable after the story.
- **Per-trophy counters** — track progress toward PSN's own targets
  (250 stunts, 100 WAiNK scans, 5 bank vaults, …).
- **Demo seeds** — `?demo=1` loads a mid-game save, `?demo=2` a late-game
  save, for trying the tracker without touching your real progress.
- **Five unsolved trophies** — *In The Beginning…*, *Capital Offense*,
  *Bad Luck, I Guess*, *The Truth Is Out There* and *Ten Pin* had no confirmed
  unlock in any published guide when this was written. They say so here
  instead of guessing.

## Use it

Serve `app/` with any static server, or run `node serve.ts` and open
<http://localhost:8080>. It works with the network off: no CDN, no external
fonts, no requests at runtime.

## Sources

Official names and descriptions are PlayStation's list, cross-checked on
PlanetGTA6, Exophase and PSNProfiles on 2026-11-21. Guides are written for
this page; each trophy cites its sources beside its guide (mainly the
PowerPyx trophy guide & roadmap and the PlanetGTA6 list).

Fan-made guide, not affiliated with Rockstar Games. No official artwork or
logos are used.
